# PLAN CHI TIET - TRANG MENU

## 1. Vai tro trang

Trang Menu la noi hien thi danh sach do uong va do an o muc demo giao dien.

Trang nay can:

* Ro rang
* De xem
* Co hinh anh dep
* Nhin co cam giac "day la menu cua quan"

Khong can he thong quan ly menu that.

---

## 2. Muc tieu hien thi

Nguoi xem can:

* Nhin thay cac nhom mon
* Xem ten mon, gia, mo ta ngan
* Thay anh minh hoa neu co
* De bam quay lai lien he hoac nhan quan

---

## 3. Bo cuc de xuat

1. Header
2. Banner trang Menu

4. Mon noi bat trong menu

## 4. Noi dung tung khu

### Banner

```txt
Menu Green Bean

Kham pha ca phe, tra va nhung mon nuoc duoc yeu thich tai Green Bean Coffee & Tea.
```

### Nhom mon

Goi y:

* Tat ca
* Coffee
* Tea
* Milk Tea
* Signature
* Smoothie
* Cake / Snacks

### Mon noi bat trong menu

Chi can 3-4 mon duoc nhan manh o dau trang.

### Danh sach mon

Moi mon gom:

* Anh
* Ten mon
* Gia
* Mo ta ngan
* Tag neu can

Vi du:

```txt
Green Bean Coffee - 35.000d
Ca phe dam vi, hau ngot nhe.

Peach Tea - 39.000d
Tra dao thanh mat, de uong.
```

### Ghi chu nho

```txt
Hinh anh chi mang tinh minh hoa. Menu va gia co the thay doi theo thoi diem.
```

### CTA cuoi trang

* Nhan Facebook
* Chi duong

## 5. Cach lam phu hop voi HTML demo

Uu tien 1 trong 2 cach:

### Cach don gian nhat

Hardcode danh sach mon trong HTML.

### Cach linh hoat hon

Dung 1 mang du lieu nho trong JS de render ra card mon.

Luu y:

* Day la tuy chon, khong bat buoc.
* Neu du lieu it, lam truc tiep trong HTML se nhanh hon.

---

## 6. Filter va tuong tac

Filter chi nen la tuong tac front-end don gian:

* Bam tab de hien/ an card
* Hoac bam tab de cuon den nhom mon

Khong can:

* Tim kiem nang cao
* Sap xep gia
* Phan trang

---

## 7. Hinh anh

Trong giai doan demo:

* Co the dung anh that neu da co
* Hoac dung placeholder dong bo

Neu thieu anh:

* Dung 1 anh mac dinh cho card mon

Khong dua vao scope web:

* Upload anh
* Tool generate anh
* Quy trinh xu ly anh ben trong website

Neu muon note, ghi:

> Hinh anh duoc chuan bi thu cong ben ngoai website; giao dien chi co nhiem vu hien thi.
