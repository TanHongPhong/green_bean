# PLAN CHI TIET - TRANG CHU

## 1. Vai tro trang

Trang chu la noi tao an tuong dau tien va dan nguoi xem den cac trang con lai.

Trang nay chi can:

* Dep
* Ro thuong hieu
* Co cam giac quan co that
* Co du nut dieu huong de xem them

Khong can lam qua nhieu tuong tac.

---

## 2. Muc tieu hien thi

Nguoi xem vao trang chu can nhin ra nhanh:

* Ten quan la gi
* Quan theo phong cach nao
* Co nhung mon nao dang chu y
* Khong gian quan ra sao
* Can bam dau de xem menu hoac lien he

---

## 3. Bo cuc de xuat

1. Header
2. Hero banner
3. Gioi thieu ngan
4. Mon noi bat
5. Preview menu

7. Uu dai ngan

---

## 4. Noi dung tung khu

### Header

* Logo
* Ten quan
* Menu dieu huong
* Nut: `Nhan Facebook`, `Chi duong`

### Hero banner

Noi dung chinh:

* Anh nen lon
* Ten quan
* Slogan
* Mo ta ngan
* 2 CTA

Text demo:

```txt
Green Bean Coffee & Tea

Mot goc ca phe xanh cho ngay nhe nhang hon.

Thuong thuc ca phe, tra va nhung mon nuoc de chiu trong khong gian am ap, gan gui.
```

CTA:

* Xem menu
* Kham pha khong gian

### Gioi thieu ngan

* 1 doan mo ta ngan
* 3 diem noi bat

Goi y 3 diem:

* Khong gian thoai mai
* Do uong de thuong thuc
* Hop hoc tap va gap go

### Mon noi bat

* 3-4 card la du
* Moi card gom: anh, ten, gia, tag

### Preview menu

* Hien thi 2-3 nhom mon
* Moi nhom 2-3 mon mau
* Co nut sang trang `menu.html`

### Preview khong gian

* 3-4 anh
* Co nut sang trang `space.html`

### Uu dai ngan

* 1 banner hoac 2 card
* Chi la noi dung minh hoa

### Lien he nhanh

* Dia chi demo
* Gio mo cua
* Hotline
* Nut goi / Facebook / chi duong

---

## 5. Hanh vi demo

Chi can cac hanh vi sau:

* Bam menu de sang trang hoac cuon toi section
* Bam CTA de sang trang lien quan
* Menu mobile dong/mo

Khong can:

* Slider phuc tap
* Video hero
* Animation nang

---

## 6. Giao dien de xuat

* Hero chieu cao lon, anh ro
* Khoang trang thoang
* Card bo goc vua phai
* Mau dong bo voi toan site
* Mobile uu tien nut bam lon, de nhin

## 7. Note scope

* Mon noi bat o trang chu chi la preview
