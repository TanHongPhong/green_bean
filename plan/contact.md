## 4. Noi dung tung khu

### Banner

```txt
Lien he Green Bean

Ghe Green Bean Coffee & Tea hoac nhan cho chung toi de duoc ho tro nhanh nhat.
```

### Thong tin lien he

Noi dung de xuat:

* Dia chi: Dang cap nhat
* Gio mo cua: 07:00 - 22:00
* Hotline: 0900 000 000
* Facebook: fanpage demo
* Google Maps: link demo hoac placeholder

### Ban do

* Co the dung iframe tinh neu co dia chi
* Neu chua co, dung block placeholder "Ban do se cap nhat"

### Form demo

Chi can cac truong:

* Ho ten
* So dien thoai
* Noi dung

Nut gui khi bam chi hien:

```txt
Cam on ban! Day la form demo, thong tin chua duoc gui that.
```
