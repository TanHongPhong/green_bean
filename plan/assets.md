## 5. Nhung anh con thieu de demo dep hon

De website len dung chat quan cafe, nen bo sung them it nhat:

* 1 anh mat tien rong hon
* 2-3 anh khong gian ben trong that su
* 1-2 anh ban ghe / quay pha che
* 1 anh quay pha che
* 1-2 anh goc check-in

Ly do:

* Bo anh hien tai rat on cho thuong hieu va san pham
* Nhung van chua du manh de tao mot trang "Khong gian quan" theo huong noi that that

Tong toi thieu de demo on:

* **8-10 anh**
